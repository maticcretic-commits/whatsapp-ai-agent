Acme Cloud Storage — Customer FAQ
(Sample knowledge base for the WhatsApp agent demo. Fictional company.)

What is Acme Cloud Storage?
Acme Cloud Storage is a fictional file-hosting service used to demonstrate
this WhatsApp agent. It lets you store, sync, and share files across devices.

Pricing
The Starter plan costs $9 per month and includes 100 GB of storage.
The Business plan costs $29 per month and includes 2 TB of storage and
priority support. Annual billing saves 20% on both plans.

Refund policy
We offer a 30-day money-back guarantee on all paid plans. To request a
refund, contact support with your account email and order number.

Support hours
Our support team is available Monday to Friday, 9am to 6pm IST, via live
chat and email. Business plan customers also get phone support.

Data retention
Deleted files are kept in the trash for 30 days before permanent deletion.
You can restore them yourself at any time during that window.
